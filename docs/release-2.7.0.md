# 2.7.0 — ancestry expansion

The Character Workshop and Foundry system now use book v16.7.0
(`f3fb98ca03b046335fcc730c1b05689bebf091b4`): ten ancestries, fifty traits,
and the shared Vision and Ancestry Procedures rules.

Choose High Elf, Wood Elf, Hill Dwarf, Deep Dwarf, Tallfolk, Gnome, Goblin,
Oath-Bound, Human or Halfling. Each ancestry offers five traits; choose two.
Existing Elf and Dwarf character identities remain compatible with Wood Elf
and Hill Dwarf. Saved drafts use the current rules automatically.

Permanent HP, Speed and carrying benefits feed character calculations.
Adaptable grants its additional DP at every level, including level 0.
A Foot in Two Worlds chooses another ancestry, one of its traits and one
DA-approved native language. Older incomplete choices remain visible for completion;
no replacement trait or language is chosen on a player's behalf.
Small Target contributes its defense Boon, or an attacking player's Bane.
Low-light vision and seismic vision show their printed ratings and refer to
shared rules; complete darkness does not grant vision. Conditional trait
Boons and sense applicability remain player/DA judgments.

The Debt Comes Due preserves an ordinary attribute check's original attribute and modifiers,
pays 1 HP while leaving at least 1 HP, and tracks that unavailable HP through
recovery. Next dawn clears the debt without healing the character.
Oath-Bound otherwise use ordinary hero recovery procedures. In Foundry, coordinated
ability, attack, defense and crafting results use the manual Debt procedure in
Recovery; their settled combat receipts are not rewritten. Ordinary attribute
checks have a chat reroll button. An interrupted native roll reserves its payment
and requires the DA to recover its retained dice rather than roll or pay again.

Committed world Actors and personal world content are not rebuilt from the
new catalog. Review their ancestry traits against the current book when
updating existing campaign characters.

Validation and publication evidence are recorded in the #259 acceptance record.
