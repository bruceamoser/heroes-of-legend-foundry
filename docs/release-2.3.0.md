# Heroes of Legend 2.3.0

- Owned unlinked token characters can Add/Remove level and develop normally.
- Language picker text is readable. Roll ability sections start closed and list the
  initiating ability and relevant printed Boon/Bane references. Reaction cards show
  their trigger before the effect.
- Encounters track completed participants and rounds without action counters or side
  ordering. Finish resolves canonical condition damage, then temporary decay exactly
  once; starred, maintained and binary conditions keep their persistence.
- Conditions combine into one displayed rating and their names open rules. Creature
  zero HP adds native Dead; positive HP removes only the automatic Dead effect.
- Ordinary equipment names/descriptions are editable; equipment arrangements are
  silent and do not police hand counts. Supply rolls explicitly report size changes
  and depletion, and supplies offer a manual Deplete correction.
- Damage cards state applied resistance. Wound names open full details; summaries
  state actual modifiers. Matching physical/mental wound penalties start selected
  in rolls and remain visible and overridable for the activity.
- Header HP accepts signed corrections. Native V14 movement measurement/history
  colors routes green through Speed, yellow through twice Speed and red beyond.
- All 30 book section pages contain chapter navigation, with authored opening paragraphs where a chapter manuscript exists. The 1,018 source pages are
  unchanged. Existing world copies repair only exact untouched old blank dividers;
  edited pages remain intact.
